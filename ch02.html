Part 1: The meeting and previous years' questions

Chapter 2 of 62

Questions only you can answer

When and how long: Answer these first, one sitting.

These are the 103 questions marked [YOU: ...] in the kit: facts about you that no file on your Mac settles. Each one is listed once here, even when several files ask it.

How to use it:

  • Write the true answer under each question. "I don't know" or "I don't remember" is a fine answer; many files give a line to use then.
  • Then fix the answer text in the files listed under it.
  • Start with sections 1 to 4 (your talk and your work), then 11 (your plan), then 14 (money).
  • The kit also has 45 [FILL: ...] blanks where you write your own wording (for example your applicant number). They are not repeated here.
