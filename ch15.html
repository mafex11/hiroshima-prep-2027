The application: process, fees and dates

2. Are you eligible? Yes

  • You must be non-Japanese, live outside Japan when you apply, and meet one of the criteria by 31 Mar 2027 (p. 9).
  • For the Engineering and Informatics Programs the criteria are (3), (6) or (10). You meet (3): "a bachelor's degree after passing through a 16-year school education course in a country other than Japan". Twelve years of school plus your four-year B.E. is 16 years.
  • The preliminary evaluation (13 to 23 Oct) was only for criteria (9), (10) and (11). It does not apply to you.
  • English requirement (p. 10): a score from May 2024 onward, for example IELTS Academic 5.5 or above, or a certificate that your degree was taught in English. You have IELTS Academic 8.0 (test date 21 Sep 2026).

3. The three parts of applying

The guideline says all three must be done between Mon 2 Nov and Fri 13 Nov 2026, 17:00 JST (p. 11):

  1. Fill in the application online (UCARO).
  2. Pay the application fee, 30,000 yen.
  3. Mail the paper documents so they arrive by the deadline.

"The application via internet will not be completed when only the input of the information on the website and the payment of the application fee are done." And documents alone are not accepted either. You need all three.

Order: online first, then the documents. The guideline says "after making the application via internet, submit all the documents" (p. 16).

8. The oral exam and how you are scored (p. 19)

  • Format: an oral exam over the internet, "using remote conferencing systems such as Teams or Zoom".
  • What it tests: "academic abilities regarding knowledge in the specialized field, application capability, and problem-solving abilities".
  • Details: "will be notified on the day of the entrance examination". Nothing else is published.
  • Scoring: the oral exam is graded A, B or C. The documents are graded A, B or C. The two are judged together.
  • Misconduct stops the exam on the spot, and if found later, cancels admission with no refund of any fee (p. 20).
  • What to expect with this lab: probability and statistics, the basics of reliability models, and your own plan. Your prep: 06_Reliability_and_Stats/02_oral_exam_prep.md. Ask Prof. Zheng on Monday what to revise (question 4 in 02_Meeting_Strategy/02_questions_to_ask_him.md).

When is the enrolment fee due? Your guideline only says the admission documents "will be sent after the selection result is announced" (p. 21). It gives no date.

  • A sister guideline from the same graduate school (Transdisciplinary Science and Engineering Program, Development Science field, July 2026) says April entrants do the enrolment procedure "early of March", and the enrolment fee is paid at the enrolment procedure.
  • So expect the enrolment fee to be due in early March 2027. [CONFIRM] in the admission guide you get after 14 Dec.
  • Why it matters: NAIST's exam is on 10 Mar 2027 and its results on 15 Mar 2027. If Hiroshima's fee is due in early March, you would pay 282,000 yen before you know NAIST's result. See 04_hiroshima_vs_naist_decision.md.

10. Other points in the guideline

  • Consult your supervisor before applying (p. 8 and 20). Done: Prof. Zheng agreed on 29 Oct, and the Support Office knows.
  • That consultation "does not affect the result of the selection in any way" (p. 20). Monday's call is a planning talk, not part of the exam.
  • Special needs for the exam had to be requested 13 to 23 Oct. Not relevant to you.
  • Security export control: the university screens international students under Japan's export rules and may limit some research (p. 21). Nothing to do now.
  • Child Sexual Violence Prevention Act: at admission you will be asked to give a consent about checks for some activities such as teaching practicums (p. 21). Nothing to do now.
  • No smoking anywhere on campus since January 2020.
  • Visa: the guideline says nothing about it. [CONFIRM] in the admission guide how and when the university starts your Certificate of Eligibility for an April 2027 start.
