Hiroshima (April 2027) vs NAIST (October 2027): decide before the oral exam

When and how long: Before 24 Nov.

This page does not choose for you. It puts the facts side by side so you can decide before 24 Nov, because Prof. Zheng may ask, and the enrolment fee timing matters.

Hiroshima University NAIST
Programme Informatics and Data Science Program (Master's) Information Science (Master's)
Lab Dependable Systems Lab: Assoc. Prof. Junjun Zheng (agreed to supervise), Prof. Okamura, Prof. Dohi Software Design and Analysis Lab: Prof. Iida, Dr. Ji, Dr. Reid, Dr. Kashiwa
Your topic there Reliability: do agent runs that share one context file fail together? Which context-file rules coding agents follow
Fit Reliability modelling is the lab's core; agents are your side Context files and agent PRs are the lab's own topic
Start April 2027 October 2027
Status now Supervisor agreed; apply 2 to 13 Nov; oral exam 24 Nov to 2 Dec; results 14 Dec Lab interview done 26 Oct; waiting for the Notice of Scheduled Laboratory Assignment (around 20 Nov); apply 8 to 10 Feb; exam 10 Mar; results 15 Mar
Fees 30,000 application; 282,000 enrolment (non-refundable); 535,800 a year 30,000 application; 282,000 enrolment; 535,800 a year
Scholarship Self-funded (MEXT unlikely without publications) Self-funded (IPGP slots were full)
Maths in the exam Oral exam on specialised knowledge and problem solving Oral maths (algebra and analysis)

The timing problem

  • Hiroshima results come on 14 Dec 2026. The admission guide that follows will set the deadline to pay the 282,000 yen enrolment fee.
  • NAIST's exam is on 10 Mar 2027, results on 15 Mar.
  • If Hiroshima's payment deadline is before 15 Mar, you would have to decide on Hiroshima before you know NAIST's result. If you pay and then choose NAIST, the 282,000 yen is lost.

Questions to answer for yourself

  1. Which topic do you want to spend two years on: reliability modelling (Hiroshima) or context-file rules (NAIST)?
  2. Does starting six months earlier (April) matter to you?
  3. Which supervisor relationship feels stronger? Prof. Zheng has already said yes.
  4. Can you accept losing 282,000 yen if you pay Hiroshima and then choose NAIST?

Being fair to both labs

  • Do not tell Prof. Zheng that Hiroshima is your first choice unless it is.
  • If he asks, say you are also applying to NAIST for October, and that you will tell him your decision quickly.
  • Once you decide, tell the lab you are not choosing as soon as possible, so they can free the place.
