Part 5: Maths

Chapter 26 of 54

Math syllabus and study plan (13 to 23 Nov 2026)

When and how long: Read first. Then about 90 minutes a day, 13 to 23 Nov.

The meeting. An online meeting with the professors on Teams or Zoom, some day between 24 Nov and 2 Dec 2026. About 15 minutes of presentation, then about 15 minutes of Q&A. On 2 Nov Prof. Zheng said he thinks most of the Q&A will focus on your proposal, but that it is "maybe not limited to" the proposal (speech-to-text wording) and depends on which professors join. When you asked about maths and statistics, he did not say no. So be ready for a few math, probability or statistics questions, asked out loud, most of them about the formulas in your own plan.

The plan. About 1.5 hours a day for 11 days. Do everything out loud, on paper, as in 07_how_to_answer_math_out_loud.md. The order puts the most likely topics first, so if your meeting slot is early (24 Nov) you have still covered what matters.

How the answers were checked. Every numeric answer in files 01 to 06 was computed on 12 Nov 2026 with Python and recomputed on 13 Nov 2026, and the formulas in your plan were also checked by simulation (file 05 has the table). Each file says what was checked. On 13 Nov every problem number in the day plan below was also checked against the files: all of them exist. Items that I am not fully sure of are marked [CHECK]; the list is at the end of this page.

3. Day-by-day plan (about 90 minutes a day)

Every day: 10 minutes of warm-up (say five definitions out loud in one sentence each), then the problems, then 5 minutes writing your mistakes in an error log (date, problem, what went wrong, the fix). For each problem: cover the solution, say the plan, solve on paper out loud, check, then read the solution.
