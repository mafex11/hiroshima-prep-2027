How to read the table

  • "No: paywalled" means closed access with no legal free copy found. "No: blocked" means the site refused the download script or was down; where a free copy exists, open the landing page in a normal browser. "No: not found" means no online copy or record was found.
  • Summaries come only from the abstract (or, for a few downloaded papers, the abstract or summary paragraph inside the PDF). If there was no abstract, the cell says "No abstract found".
  • Relevance is high, medium or low against your proposal (RQ1 shared failures and voting, RQ2 failures beyond the tests and boundary inputs, and the field-data NHPP model). References like [1] are the reference numbers in your proposal.
  • Japanese titles carry an English gloss in [EN: ...]; those glosses are my translations, not official titles.
