Hiroshima oral exam prep

Your study book for the online oral exam at Hiroshima University (Informatics and Data Science Program, April 2027). About 15 minutes of presentation, then about 15 minutes of questions, on a day between 24 Nov and 2 Dec.

  • Every chapter starts with when to read it and for how long. Chapter 1 has the full day-by-day plan.
  • Tick Mark as done at the end of a chapter. Progress is saved on this device only.
  • The three mock exams have play buttons, so you can hear each question.
  • Your research plan: PDF. Your slides: PDF.
  • No AI help during the exam. Close Natively and every assistant before you join.
