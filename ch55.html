Chapter 55 of 61

Meeting day checklist: your meeting with the Hiroshima professors

When and how long: Read on 22 Nov, use on the day.

Online, on Teams or Zoom. The Program sets the day, between Tuesday 24 November and Wednesday 2 December 2026. Prof. Zheng will send you the date, the time and the other details (Support Office email of 12 Nov).

1. Time

JST = IST + 3 hours 30 minutes. Expect the time in JST. Subtract 3:30 for India.

JST IST
09:00 05:30
10:00 06:30
11:00 07:30
13:00 09:30
14:00 10:30
15:00 11:30
16:00 12:30
17:00 13:30

When the email arrives, write it here: date __________, time ______ JST = ______ IST, platform ______, link in the email from ______.

Join 10 minutes early.

2. In the days before

  • [ ] Applicant number and the UCARO card. On UCARO after 12:00 JST on 19 November 2026 (08:30 IST), planned. Download the card from UCARO, save the PDF, print one copy, and write your applicant number on your desk paper. [YOU: After 12:00 JST on 19 Nov, open the UCARO card: does it show PANDIT / SUDHANSH or SUDHANSHU PANDIT?]
  • [ ] Teams and Zoom. As of 12 November neither is installed in /Applications. Install both desktop apps. Allow Screen and System Audio Recording, Camera and Microphone for each (System Settings > Privacy & Security), then quit and reopen them.
  • [ ] Trial call on each app with a friend: camera, microphone, screen sharing of the deck PDF, and one call over your phone hotspot.
  • [ ] The deck PDF. 02_Presentation_15min/Sudhanshu_Pandit_Hiroshima_presentation.pdf opens in Preview, and you have shared it once in each app (see PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md, section 3).
  • [ ] Print your research plan (04_My_Proposal/Research_Plan.md). You will ask at the start whether you may keep it on the desk.
  • [ ] Three timed run-throughs done (PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md, section 2).
  • [ ] Plan the room: quiet, a door you can close, light in front of you, a plain wall behind you. Tell your family the time.

3. Your desk, one hour before

  • [ ] Quiet room, door closed, a note on the door.
  • [ ] Wired power. The laptop is plugged in, not on battery.
  • [ ] Camera at eye level. Put the laptop on books. Light in front of your face, not behind you.
  • [ ] Internet working. Backup: phone hotspot switched on, charged, and tested.
  • [ ] Wired earphones with a microphone, if you have them. They stop echo.
  • [ ] The UCARO card printout, or your applicant number on paper.
  • [ ] Printed research plan, face down, until they say yes.
  • [ ] Pen and blank paper, for noting a long question.
  • [ ] A glass of water.
  • [ ] Do Not Disturb on (Control Centre > Focus). Slack, Mail, Messages and WhatsApp quit.
  • [ ] The deck PDF open in Preview on slide 1. Nothing else open on the screen, since screen sharing can show it.

4. Ten minutes before

  • [ ] Open the meeting link from the meeting email. Join 10 minutes early.
  • [ ] Check your camera and microphone in the app's preview. Your display name: Sudhanshu Pandit, or the format the meeting email asks for. [YOU: When Prof. Zheng's meeting email arrives: what display name and what contact address for a dropped call does it give?]
  • [ ] Sit up, smile, drink some water.

5. At the start

Greet them, then ask about your printed plan before you present:

Hello. Thank you for this opportunity, and thank you for your time today. My name is Sudhanshu Pandit. ... Before I start, may I keep a printed copy of my research plan on my desk?

If they say no, put it away: "Of course."

Then: "May I share my screen?" and "Can you see my slides?" The full words are in 02_Presentation_15min/FULL_SCRIPT.md. If they give you a time that differs from 15 minutes, use the 10- or 5-minute version (PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md, section 5).

6. If something goes wrong

  • Connection drops: rejoin at once with the same link. If your internet is down, switch to the phone hotspot and rejoin. Then: "Sorry, I lost my connection. Shall I continue from slide ...?"
  • You cannot get back in within a few minutes: email the contact named in the meeting email. Template 9 in 04_email_templates.md is a start; send it to that contact, not only Prof. Zheng. The Support Office address in your templates is kou-gaku-daigakuin@office.hiroshima-u.ac.jp. [YOU: When Prof. Zheng's meeting email arrives: what display name and what contact address for a dropped call does it give?]
  • Screen sharing fails twice: "May I present without slides?" Then use the cue lines.
  • You did not catch a question: "Sorry, could you say that again, please?"

7. After the call

  • [ ] Wait until they end the meeting, or say "Thank you, goodbye" and leave after they do.
  • [ ] Within the hour, write down every question you were asked and how you answered. It helps for NAIST.
  • [ ] Now reopen your other apps.
  • [ ] Hiroshima's decision: planned for Monday 14 December 2026, sent by email to the accepted applicants.
