Chapter 39 of 45

Exam day checklist: Hiroshima University oral examination

When and how long: Read on 22 Nov, use on the day.

Online, on Teams or Zoom. The Program sets the day, between Tuesday 24 November and Wednesday 2 December 2026, and tells you by email. The guideline says: "Details of the oral examination will be notified on the day of the entrance examination."

The firm rule: no AI tools of any kind

Before you join, quit Natively and every AI assistant, copilot or transcription app. Keep them closed until the call has ended.

The guideline (section VII.2) says: "If you engage in misconduct, you will be instructed to discontinue the examination and will not be permitted to take any subsequent examinations. Furthermore, if misconduct is discovered after admission or enrollment, admission and/or enrollment will be revoked." So one open app can cost you the place, even after you have started in April.

Quit all of these that are running on this Mac:

  • [ ] Natively. It starts from ~/Applications/Start Natively.command. Close its window, then quit the Terminal window that started it.
  • [ ] Minimi (/Applications/Minimi.app) and MaxMi. They capture what is on your screen.
  • [ ] ChatGPT and Claude desktop apps.
  • [ ] Claude Code, Codex and T3 Code sessions, including any running in tmux or in the background. Quit Cursor, Warp and Raycast too.
  • [ ] Any meeting note-taker or transcription app (for example Granola, Fireflies, Otter), and macOS Live Captions.
  • [ ] Browser extensions with AI features. Use a clean browser window, or better, the desktop Teams or Zoom app.
  • [ ] Do not turn on any AI, transcription or recap feature inside Teams or Zoom yourself.
  • [ ] Your phone: no assistant apps open. It stays face down, on Do Not Disturb, and is only your backup hotspot.

Check that they are really gone. Open Activity Monitor and search for each name. Or run this in Terminal, then quit Terminal:

ps aux | grep -i -E "natively|minimi|maxmi|chatgpt|claude|codex|cursor|raycast|granola|t3 code" | grep -v grep

It should print nothing. Then look at the menu bar at the top right of the screen: no AI app icons.

Nobody else in the room. No second screen with notes.

When the email arrives, write it here: date __________, time ______ JST = ______ IST, platform ______, link in the email from ______.

Join 10 minutes early.

2. In the days before

  • [ ] Examinee number and Examination Card. On UCARO after 12:00 JST on 19 November 2026 (08:30 IST), planned. The guideline says: "Please be sure to download and prepare your Examination Card from UCARO so that you can recognize it on the day of the examination." Download it, save the PDF, print one copy, and write the examinee number on your desk paper. [CONFIRM: your 10 November UCARO check says the card shows the name as PANDIT / SUDHANSH because of a 14-character limit. Check that this is still how it looks.]
  • [ ] Teams and Zoom. As of 12 November neither is installed in /Applications. Install both desktop apps. Allow Screen and System Audio Recording, Camera and Microphone for each (System Settings > Privacy & Security), then quit and reopen them.
  • [ ] Test call on each app with a friend: camera, microphone, screen sharing of the deck PDF, and one call over your phone hotspot.
  • [ ] The deck PDF. 02_Presentation_15min/Sudhanshu_Pandit_Hiroshima_oral_exam.pdf opens in Preview, and you have shared it once in each app (see PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md, section 3).
  • [ ] Print your research plan (04_My_Proposal/Research_Plan_SUBMITTED_Sudhanshu_Pandit.pdf). You will ask at the start whether you may keep it on the desk.
  • [ ] Three timed run-throughs done (PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md, section 2).
  • [ ] Plan the room: quiet, a door you can close, light in front of you, a plain wall behind you. Tell your family the time.

3. Your desk, one hour before

  • [ ] Quiet room, door closed, a note on the door.
  • [ ] Wired power. The laptop is plugged in, not on battery.
  • [ ] Camera at eye level. Put the laptop on books. Light in front of your face, not behind you.
  • [ ] Internet working. Backup: phone hotspot switched on, charged, and tested.
  • [ ] Wired earphones with a microphone, if you have them. They stop echo.
  • [ ] Photo ID (passport) on the desk. [CONFIRM if they ask for it: the guideline uses your UCARO photo for identification, but have your passport ready in case they ask to see an ID on camera.]
  • [ ] Examination Card printout or your examinee number on paper.
  • [ ] Printed research plan, face down, until they say yes.
  • [ ] Pen and blank paper, for noting a long question.
  • [ ] A glass of water.
  • [ ] Do Not Disturb on (Control Centre > Focus). Slack, Mail, Messages and WhatsApp quit.
  • [ ] The deck PDF open in Preview on slide 1. Nothing else open on the screen.
  • [ ] The AI rule above: all checked.

4. Ten minutes before

  • [ ] Open the meeting link from the official email. Join 10 minutes early.
  • [ ] Check your camera and microphone in the app's preview. Your display name: Sudhanshu Pandit. [CONFIRM: use another format if the exam notice asks for one, for example your examinee number.]
  • [ ] Sit up, smile, drink some water.

5. At the start

Greet them, then ask about your printed plan before you present:

Hello. Thank you for this opportunity, and thank you for your time today. My name is Sudhanshu Pandit. ... Before I start, may I keep a printed copy of my research plan on my desk?

If they say no, put it out of sight at once: "Of course."

Then: "May I share my screen?" and "Can you see my slides?" The full words are in 02_Presentation_15min/FULL_SCRIPT.md. If they give you a time that differs from 15 minutes, use the 10- or 5-minute version (PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md, section 5).

6. If something goes wrong

  • Connection drops: rejoin at once with the same link. If your internet is down, switch to the phone hotspot and rejoin. Then: "Sorry, I lost my connection. Shall I continue from slide ...?"
  • You cannot get back in within a few minutes: email the contact in the exam notice. Template 9 in 04_email_templates.md is a start; send it to the exam contact, not only Prof. Zheng. [CONFIRM: use the address the exam notice gives. The Support Office address in your templates is kou-gaku-daigakuin@office.hiroshima-u.ac.jp.]
  • Screen sharing fails twice: "May I present without slides?" Then use the cue lines.
  • You did not catch a question: "Sorry, could you say that again, please?"

7. After the call

  • [ ] Wait until they end the meeting, or say "Thank you, goodbye" and leave after they do.
  • [ ] Within the hour, write down every question you were asked and how you answered. It helps for NAIST.
  • [ ] Only now reopen your other apps.
  • [ ] Results: planned for Monday 14 December 2026. The guideline says they are sent "to the accepted applicants via e-mail".
