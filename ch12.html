Day-of checklist: Monday 2 November, Microsoft Teams

When and how long: Monday morning.

What this file is: everything practical for Monday's call: Teams setup, timing, what to keep on screen, the first minute, the close, and what to do if something breaks.

The call: Monday 2 Nov 2026, 17:00 to 17:30 JST = 13:30 to 14:00 IST, Microsoft Teams, with Assoc. Prof. Junjun Zheng (Prof. Dohi and Prof. Okamura may join). The link is in his email of 30 Oct. Open it from that email. Do not forward it or paste it anywhere.

2. Teams setup (do it on Saturday or Sunday, not on Monday)

2.1 App or browser

You can join in either of two ways. Both are free. You do not need a work account.

  • Teams app for Mac (better for you). Install it from Microsoft's website (search "Microsoft Teams download"). This Mac has 16 GB of memory, and Chrome has frozen under load before, so the app is the safer choice for this call.
  • Browser. Click the link and choose "Continue on this browser". Use Chrome or Edge. Safari may not support everything.

Test the link on Sunday: open his email, click the link, and go as far as the screen that says "Join now". Check your name, camera and mic there. Do not press "Join now" on Sunday. Close the window.

If the app asks you to sign in, you can sign in with a free Microsoft account, or join as a guest by typing your name. Either is fine.

2.2 Your name

  • [ ] Display name: Sudhanshu Pandit. Not "mafex", not an email address, not anyone else's name.
  • [ ] If you join as a guest, type "Sudhanshu Pandit" in the name box.
  • [ ] If you sign in with a Microsoft account, check the name on the account.

2.3 Mac permissions

  • [ ] System Settings, Privacy and Security: allow Camera, Microphone, and Screen Recording (on newer macOS: "Screen and System Audio Recording") for Teams. If you use Chrome instead, allow them for Chrome.
  • [ ] macOS asks you to quit and reopen the app after you allow screen recording. Do it now, not during the call.

2.4 Test everything once

  • [ ] A test call. If the Teams app shows a "test call" option in its settings, use it. Otherwise start a Teams meeting with your brother, or record yourself for 10 seconds with QuickTime or Photo Booth.
  • [ ] Share one window: open the slides PDF in Preview, click Share in Teams, pick the Preview window. Check the text is readable. Stop sharing.
  • [ ] Practise switching between the slides window and the proposal window while sharing.

2.5 The lobby

He may need to let you in. If Teams says something like "Someone in the meeting should let you in soon", wait calmly. If nothing happens by 13:35 IST, see section 8.

3. Camera, sound, light, room

  • [ ] Camera at eye level. Put the laptop on books.
  • [ ] Head and shoulders in the frame, a little space above your head.
  • [ ] Look at the camera when you speak. Put his video window at the top of the screen, just under the camera.
  • [ ] Light in front of you, not behind. 13:30 IST is daytime, so a window in front of you works.
  • [ ] Plain, tidy wall behind you. No virtual background.
  • [ ] Wired earphones with a mic if you have them. Bluetooth can lag or die.
  • [ ] Quiet room with a door. Tell your family the time. A note on the door: "On a call until 14:15".
  • [ ] Fan, AC and TV off if they are loud.

  • [ ] Backup internet: your phone hotspot, tested once with the laptop on Sunday. Phone charged above 80%.

  • [ ] Laptop on the charger.
  • [ ] A short email ready on your phone (Gmail draft, not sent), in his thread: > Dear Prof. Zheng, I am sorry, my connection dropped. I am rejoining now. Sudhanshu Pandit

8. The first minute

When the call connects:

"Hello, Prof. Zheng. Can you hear me well?"

When he answers:

"Thank you very much for making time for me today, and for agreeing to supervise my research."

If Prof. Dohi or Prof. Okamura is there: greet each by name. "Good to meet you, Prof. Okamura." "Thank you for joining, Prof. Dohi, and thank you for introducing me to Prof. Zheng."

If he asks you to introduce yourself: the 1-minute intro from 08_Presentation/FULL_SCRIPT.md. Then stop and wait.

If sound or video is wrong:

"Sorry, I think my audio isn't clear. Let me fix it." Check the selected mic and speaker in Teams. If it still fails after 30 seconds, leave and rejoin.

Do not: start with a long speech, start in Japanese, or apologise for your English.

9. The close

Say back what you agreed:

"So, I'll send you the Consent Form today, and the updated research plan by [day]. You'll [give the signed form to the Support Office / send me a scan]. Is that right?"

Then:

"Thank you very much for your time today. I look forward to working with you."

  • Optional, only if it feels natural: "Arigatou gozaimashita." (Thank you very much.)
  • Smile, give a small nod, and wait. Let him end the call or leave first. If everyone waits, say "Thank you, goodbye" once more and leave.

11. If something goes wrong

Problem What to do
Internet drops Switch to the phone hotspot and rejoin. If it takes more than 1 minute, send the "rejoining now" email from your phone.
Laptop freezes Join from your phone. Apologise once, briefly, and carry on without slides.
Power cut The laptop battery keeps you going. Switch to the hotspot if the router dies.
Screen share fails Stop after 30 seconds. "Sorry, let me just explain it without slides." Give the 2-minute version.
You can't hear a question "Sorry, could you say that again, please?" Asking twice is fine.
You don't follow a question "Do you mean ...?" and say what you think he asked.
You don't know the answer "I don't know that yet. I'd start by reading ..., and I'd be glad if you could point me to the right material."
Someone walks in "Excuse me for one second." Close the door, come back, carry on.
He is not there at 13:35 IST Stay in the meeting. At 13:40 IST, send a short email in his thread: "Dear Prof. Zheng, I am in the Teams meeting now and will wait. Please let me know if another time is better. Sudhanshu Pandit". Wait until 13:50 IST.
You are late (should not happen) Email at once in his thread, one line, with the minute you will join.
